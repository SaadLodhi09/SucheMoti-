The Suche Moti Purity Standard

Understanding the distinction between genuine organic nacre and commercial synthetic imitations.

01

Luster & Glow

Deep, mirror-like optical refraction created by hundreds of concentric aragonite calcium carbonate layers.

02

Solid Nacre

Solid organic crystal deposition guaranteeing lifelong durability and resistance to discoloration.

03

Surface Purity

Subtle natural fingerprint marks that definitively prove organic origin rather than machine-molded smooth plastic.

04

Artisan Knotting

Individually double-knotted on 100% pure silk thread by master jewelers to prevent friction rubbing.
